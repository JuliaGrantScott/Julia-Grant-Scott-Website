01 · The Festival

A homecoming, not a festival.

Colibri Spirit Festival is one of the most extraordinary gatherings in the world — a six-day consciousness-raising journey nestled on the sacred island of Corfu, Greece, overlooking the crystal-clear waters of the Mediterranean Sea.

What makes Colibri truly unique is the rare alchemy it holds: a pristine, high-vibration tribe of people from every corner of the world, united in a shared devotion to depth, beauty, and transformation.

Here, profound ancient wisdom meets living art — through music, ceremony, movement, healing, and sacred gathering.

It is a homecoming."

02 · About the Festival

A six-day journey of
connection & celebration

From ancestral rituals to musical alchemy, from healing arts to movement medicine — every offering is woven with care, designed to open the heart and reconnect each person to spirit, to the land, and to one another.

What sets Colibri apart is the quality of its people. This is a gathering of genuine seekers — artists, healers, wisdom keepers, and everyday mystics — who arrive not as an audience, but as co-creators.

Indigenous families, world-class musicians, somatic guides, and transformational teachers share their medicine side by side, in a space of deep mutual respect. The result is something rare: a crystal-clear, high-vibration field where real transformation becomes not just possible, but inevitable.

Nestled directly by the ocean with breathtaking sunrise and sunset views, Colibri Farm offers a setting that nourishes before a single session begins. This is where Julia will offer her Clarity Breathwork Session — a portal within a portal.

05 · Your Guide

Julia Grant-Scott

Julia Grant-Scott is a sound and embodiment artist carrying heritage from the vast lands of Central Asia. Originally from Kazakhstan, she often reminds that "the lotus grows from mud" — as life led her through plenty of it. This very ground became the foundation for her own transformation.

Her journey weaves together Ashtanga Vinyasa Yoga, dance, breathwork and sound — each one shaping her relationship with life and deepening her presence. Today, Julia creates immersive spaces for those who are ready to meet themselves fully — through the discipline of the body, the intelligence of the breath, the liberation of movement, and the prayer of sound.

Not as separate practices, but as one unified path back to yourself.

Julia is lovingly called Brahmama, inspired by the Indian concept of Nada Brahma — "Sound is God, and God is Sound." A name that holds both reverence and playfulness, much like the work she offers.

Julia's work is for those who are ready to listen — not just with their ears, but with their whole being.
