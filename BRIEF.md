# BRIEF — juliascott.com

The website project scope. Read this with `BRAND.md` for full context.

---

## North Star

`juliascott.com` is the **home base** for Julia Grant Scott's sovereign brand. Its job is to fill her **6 retreats in 2026** (June, July, August, September, October, + Paris TBD) with 10–15 paying participants each. Retreats are the primary revenue vehicle. Everything else — Instagram, clothing, 1:1, sound work — feeds the retreat funnel.

**Ticket math:** ~€700/person × 10–15 people = €7,000–€10,500 per retreat. 6 retreats = ~€42,000–€63,000 ceiling for the year from retreats alone.

The Feeling Metric: *Energy is flowing. People find me. The channel is clear and stable. Filling retreats does not feel like a struggle.*

---

## Strategic context — the brand pivot

Julia is transitioning **away** from being associated primarily with John Scott Yoga (where her offerings have lived) **toward** Julia Scott as a sovereign, independent brand. This site is the centrepiece of that pivot.

It must:

- Feel **distinct** from John Scott Yoga in voice and aesthetic.
- Carry over no visual or copy debt from the John Scott site.
- Read as Julia's voice — her devotional dharma — not as a "yoga teacher's website".

---

## Audience

Primary: **The Tired Seeker** (see `BRAND.md`). Outwardly successful, spiritually literate, exhausted from modalities, ready for remembrance rather than more healing.

Secondary: yoga practitioners who already know Julia from John Scott retreats and want to follow her sovereign work.

Geography: Europe-leaning (retreats are Sardinia, Andalusia, Poland, Switzerland, Paris). English-language site.

---

## Pages — initial scope

Minimum viable site:

1. **Home** — felt-sense entry. Om glyph. One core sentence of remembrance. CTA to retreats. Glimpse of Julia.
2. **About / Origin** — Julia's story as canvas, not subject. From Poland → theatre → Buddhism → "gremlin room" → Bali surrender → remembrance. End: who she is now and the mission.
3. **Retreats** — listing of all 6 retreats for 2026. For each: dates, location, format, price, pillars covered, CTA to book / enquire.
4. **Philosophy** — the four pillars (Yoga, Sound, Breathwork, Self-Realization), the Full Cup conviction, what Julia believes that most of wellness gets wrong.
5. **Sound & Movement** — Julia's most distinctive gift. Voice, music, embodied movement. Embedded videos / audio if available.
6. **Contact / Enquire** — simple form. Name, email, message, optional retreat interest. No noise.

Future pages (post-launch, lower priority):

- 1:1 Sessions
- Clothing (daughter brand)
- Journal / writings
- Press / aligned partners

---

## Site behaviours

- Mobile-first. Most retreat seekers will land via Instagram → DM link → mobile browser.
- Direct booking pathway from any retreat card to enquire/book in ≤2 taps.
- Calendly or similar for 1:1 enquiry once that pillar launches.
- Form submissions go to Julia's primary email (confirm exact email before launch).
- Speed: under 3s mobile load. Compressed images. No heavy animations on first paint.

## Functional requirements

- Booking / enquiry form on retreat detail.
- Newsletter signup (or just mailing-list form) — single field, single CTA.
- Instagram link (channel: @[handle to confirm], 28k followers).
- Social proof: testimonials from prior retreat participants — collect 6–10 before launch.

## Out of scope (for v1)

- E-commerce for clothing (clothing is informal at retreats only in 2026).
- YouTube embed grid (not a 2026 priority).
- Spotify / SoundCloud integration (3-year vision, not now).
- Multi-language (English only for v1).

---

## Channel positioning

- **Instagram (28k followers)** — main awareness channel. 3–5 posts/week, three pillars: Heart Transmissions, Retreat World, Sound & Movement. Drives traffic to site.
- **juliascott.com (this project)** — home base. Where the retreat funnel lives.
- **John Scott Yoga page** — current home of retreat listings. To be **transitioned** to juliascott.com — once this site is live, retreat listings move here and JSY page either delists or links across.

## Visual & UX direction

Pulled from `BRAND.md`. Summary:

- Cream `#F6E9D2` and paper `#FBF1DC` as dominant surface.
- Sindoor `#C9301E` as primary accent / Om / rule lines.
- Tiro Devanagari Hindi for display, Cormorant Garamond italic for accents, JetBrains Mono for labels.
- Flower of Life watermark as recurring quiet motif.
- Devanagari script ornaments — sparingly, sindoor at low opacity.
- No stock photography. Only Julia's own imagery / commissioned imagery.
- Layout: generous whitespace, hairline rules, uppercase mono labels, italic Cormorant captions. *Devotional, rooted, Sanskrit-knowing.*
