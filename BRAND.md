The on-brand bible. Read this before writing or editing any copy, image alt text, button label, or visual element on the site.

## 1 · Visual identity

### Colour palette

| Role | Name | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary surface | Cream | `#F6E9D2` |
| Surface 2 (paper) | Paper | `#FBF1DC` |
| Surface 3 (warm) | Warm sand | `#EFDDB8` |
| Hero accent / brand red | Sindoor | `#C9301E` |
| Sindoor depth | Sindoor dark | `#8C1F12` |
| Marigold accent | Marigold | `#E8A02A` |
| Sacred green | Tulsi | `#3E5A3A` |
| Ink (text) | Ink | `#2A1410` |

Sindoor (`#C9301E`) is the brand's signature. It carries Om-glyph, primary marks, and rule lines. Cream/paper are the dominant surface. Ink is body text. Marigold and tulsi are sparing accents — never primary.

### Typography

- **Tiro Devanagari Hindi** — display, primary serif, body when used at scale. Devotional, rooted.
- **Cormorant Garamond** (italic, light/regular) — accent script, names, captions. Used for "Julia Grant Scott" wordmark in italic.
- **JetBrains Mono** — small caps labels, metadata, numbers, code. Wide letter-spacing, low opacity, contemporary anchor.

Fonts via Google Fonts. Devanagari and Cormorant should always preload.

### Typographic detail

- **Body text** (Cormorant Garamond, light 300) — base size `19px` on desktop, line-height `1.78`. Long-form bio prose uses a fluid scale of `clamp(1.12rem, 1.42vw, 1.28rem)` with line-height `1.9`. Generous, breathing, readable on a phone in low light. Bigger feels devotional; tight feels editorial — we lean devotional.
- Section labels in JetBrains Mono `7–9px`, letter-spacing `0.4–0.5em`, uppercase, sindoor at 50–65% opacity.
- Italic descriptors (Cormorant) at 50% opacity follow most labels — *"colour ways · minimum size · clear space"* style.
- Hairline rules (`0.5px`, sindoor at 20% opacity) separate sections. Thick rule (`4px` solid sindoor) marks major chapter transitions.

### Iconography & symbols

- **Om glyph (ॐ)** — primary mark. Always sindoor on cream / cream on dark / cream on sindoor.
- **Flower of Life** — recurring watermark, sindoor or cream at 5–14% opacity.
- **Three-dot ornaments** (small · medium · small dots) flanking wordmark — visual signature. Sindoor on cream, cream on dark.
- **Devanagari script** as authentic ornament (not decoration) — small-scale, sindoor at 40–55% opacity.

### Logo / wordmark anatomy

Stack: Om glyph → wordmark "BRAHMAMA" with three-dot flanks → italic Cormorant "Julia Grant Scott" → Devanagari motto → JetBrains Mono "YOGA · SOUND · BREATH". Three approved colourways:

1. **Primary on cream** — sindoor mark on `#F6E9D2`.
2. **Reversed on dark** — cream mark on `#2A1410`.
3. **Accent on sindoor** — cream mark on `#C9301E`.

## 2 · Voice & language

### Voice characteristics

- **Invitational, not prescriptive** — opens doors, doesn't push through them.
- **Warm, direct, poetic** — speaks to the soul, not at the mind.
- **Feeling over thinking** — language that lands in the body before the brain.
- **Courageous with God-language** — never dilutes the divine into spiritual jargon.
- **Personal story as canvas** — *"I share this not so you carry my story, but so you remember your own."*

### Core language & themes

- *Changing perspective* — inviting a new way of seeing.
- *Openness, curiosity* — *"What if you asked yourself why you are here?"*
- *Deepening connection with yourself* — not improving yourself.
- *Listening, creating space, courage to be.*
- *God* — reclaimed from religion. Not outside. Inside. Always. Forever.
- *Remembrance* — not learning something new, but returning to what was always known.
- *Wholeness* — the starting point, not the destination.
- *Sound as vibration* — direct transmission of the divine.

### What Julia does NOT sound like

- Educational / teacher-tone — *"Today I am going to teach you about..."*
- Lifestyle influencer — *"Here is what I ate / what I bought / where I am"*
- Therapeutic — she does not lead people back into wounds to heal them.
- Preachy or superior — she is always equal with the reader.
- Fragmented or vague — she is specific, vivid, embodied.

### Brand personality (5 words)

Devotional · Rooted · Sanskrit-knowing · Joyful · Free.

### Anchor quotes

> "You are not your thoughts. You are not your emotions. You were always already home." — Julia Scott

> "I am whole. I can create. I am God's child. We are never separate."

The Full Cup conviction: *You are already whole. You are a full cup. This is where we begin.*

## 3 · Sensory brand world

- **Scent** — subtle, powerful incense. Rose water. Never cheap or overstimulating.
- **Visual aesthetic** — clean clothing with one special detail. Flowers as offering. Rose patterns. Altar always present. Sacred objects, deity imagery.
- **Energy** — warm, mothering. People feel safe, like children. Free and curious about themselves.
- **Sound** — always the name or sound of God as vibration. Sound as clearing, not performance.

## 5 · Audience — *The Tired Seeker*

Outwardly successful, inwardly hollow. Has the relationship, lifestyle, experiences — but everything tastes like chewing gum: flavour fades fast. Spiritually literate (therapy, plant medicine, yoga, modalities) and exhausted from seeking. Beneath the success: a wound about not having the full right to expand, receive, live.

What they want: to feel beautiful exactly as they are. To stop improving. To stop performing. To open their heart and breathe freely. To become a vessel.

They arrive as seekers. They leave remembering they were never lost.

---

## 6 · Non-negotiable brand values

- Equality between giver and receiver. No hierarchy.
- Mutual respect in every interaction.
- Authenticity over performance.
- God as universal, not religious.
- Wholeness as the starting point — never selling from "you are broken and I can fix you".

---

## 7 · Quick check before publishing any copy

Ask:

1. Does this sound invitational rather than prescriptive?
2. Does it land in the body, or only in the head?
3. Is it specific and vivid, or vague and influencer-y?
4. Is personal story used as canvas, not subject?
5. Does it begin from wholeness, not lack?
6. Would Janet Stone, Bentinho, or Jacob Collier feel kinship with it?

If any answer is "no", rewrite.
